The short version

Pausette collects nothing, sends nothing and has no network access.

What Pausette notices

Whether you are using your Mac: the number of seconds since the last key press, click or trackpad movement, the same figure macOS uses for the screen saver. Pausette does not see which keys or where you click.

Whether a microphone is in use by any app, so a break can wait for your call. It reads a single on or off flag from macOS. It never opens the microphone and never hears anything, and it does not need microphone permission.

It does not use the camera, your location, your contacts, your calendar or your files.

What is stored, and where

Inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac: your break settings and today's count of breaks. Deleting the app removes them.

Where it goes

Nowhere. Pausette has no network entitlement, which means macOS itself will not let it open a network connection. There is no server, no analytics and no account.
